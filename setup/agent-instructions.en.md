## Handouts and question sheets (ai-handout-studio)
- Make slides and HTML handouts (design docs, requirements, research findings) with the ai-handout-studio skill, and keep them in the handout list.
- When asking the user questions, confirming, or offering choices, use a question sheet from the question-sheet skill, even for a single question. A short confirmation that can be answered right away may stay in the conversation.
